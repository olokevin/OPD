BP-OPD's teacher phase is not slow -- the 35.61 s was a COLD step-1
====================================================================
2026-08-24. Triggered by "why is BP-opd teacher so slow?".
Every ES/BP ratio in docs/results/zo_opd.md before today divided by a single
cold BP step (61.86 s). ~25 s of that is one-time warm-up inside the teacher.

(1) MICROBENCHMARK of the reward path -- what compute_rm_score CAN cost
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
scripts/zo_opd/es_token_checks/bench_rm_stages.py, H100 NVL (GPU 7), warm
iteration. One micro-batch = 8 seqs x 1112 tok (= the shipped
reward.micro_batch_size_per_gpu=8); 8 micro-batches per step (64 seqs).
Teacher Keven16/Qwen3-4B-Non-Thinking-RL-Math-Step500, bf16.
Logits per micro-batch: 8896 x 151936 bf16 = 2.52 GiB.

  stage                                     ms/micro-batch   s/step (x8)   share
  transformer fwd + lm_head                          324.7          2.60   85.8%
  logits.div_(teacher_temperature)                     1.6          0.01    0.4%
  _compute_entropy_safe (logging only)                21.1          0.17    5.6%
  logprobs_from_logits                                 1.7          0.01    0.4%
  teacher top-K + overlap masks                       29.4          0.24    7.8%
  TOTAL                                              378.5          3.03  100.0%

Note: use_fused_kernels=False and compute_entropy is hard-coded True in
RewardModelWorker._forward_micro_batch, so full-vocab logits are ALWAYS
materialised -- but all the full-vocab reductions together are only 0.42 s
of a step. Wrapping the teacher in the FSDP1 CPUOffload(offload_params=True)
that fsdp_workers.py:1883 hard-wires changed nothing: 333.1 ms warm vs
324.7 ms unwrapped (world_size=1 -> FSDP falls back to NO_SHARD).

(2) THE 138-STEP RUN -- what it actually costs
-----------------------------------------------
logs/train/bp_opd_20260823_010128.log, timing_s/compute_rm_score per step:

  step        1      2      3      4   ...    138
  rm_score  29.15   3.82   3.86   4.82        3.57
  median over steps 4..138 = 3.80   (min 3.46, max 7.31)

3.80 s steady vs 3.03 s predicted -- the microbenchmark is the floor and the
run pays modest co-tenancy overhead on top. Step 1 pays the teacher's first
CPU->GPU param fetch, kernel autotune, and first-touch allocation of the
2.52 GiB logits + 2.49 GiB fp32 entropy buffers into an allocator that
already holds vLLM's 55% reservation.

WITHDRAWN:  "ES/BP 0.69x -- es_token is faster than BP-OPD"  (zo_opd.md 8.3)
WITHDRAWN:  "teacher phase 8.4x / 10x faster than BP's"      (zo_opd.md 1, 4)
UNAFFECTED: the 147.54 -> 42.67 s step (3.46x) and 129.59 -> 25.40 s decode
            (5.10x) gains -- measured cold-vs-cold on one harness; the
            gradient-cosine results; the negative learning result.

Cold penalty asymmetry is the whole story: ES 42.67 -> 37.17 s = 13% (its
teacher is the vLLM engine that just ran decode, already warm); BP 61.86 ->
25.11 s = 146% (its teacher is a separate FSDP module whose first forward of
the run happens inside the timed phase). Any future ES-vs-BP number must be a
steady-state median, not a step-1 reading.
