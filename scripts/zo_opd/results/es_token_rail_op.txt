es_token rail-op overhead: diagnosis and fused-kernel fix -- 2026-08-22
=======================================================================
H100 NVL, Qwen3-1.7B bf16, 112 matched decoder linears (qkv/o/gate_up/down x 28),
d_total = 917,504 per slot. bucket (pack_width) = 4.

PROBLEM
-------
End-to-end decode (results/zo_opd.md 2): turning rails ON cost +3.41 ms/token-step
(N=0: 2.939 -> N=1: 6.347) while 31 MORE rails cost only +3.08 ms (N=32: 9.429).
A near-fixed cost that does not scale with work = launch overhead, not arithmetic:
at N=1 the rail op touches 4 rows of <=6144 elements per layer.

NOT THE RNG
-----------
draw_noise() builds torch.Generator(device=cuda) and draws ON THE GPU; nothing is
generated on the host or shipped. The fused per-(slot,token) draw costs 0.199 ms
(sweep_decode_isolation.sh) and is paid identically at N=0, so it is not part of
the N=0 -> N=1 delta at all.

DIAGNOSIS: CUDA-GRAPH NODE COUNT
--------------------------------
bench_rail_op.py replays the rail op alone, graphed, at the real shapes:
  v0_current @ N=1 = 3.376 ms   (live decode delta was 3.41 ms -- faithful)
torch profiler, ONE decode token at N=1:
  vectorized_gather_kernel          672 calls   1207.8 us   1.80 us/call
  elementwise_kernel<128,4>         224 calls    602.9 us   2.69 us/call   (non-vectorised)
  vectorized_elementwise_kernel<8>  336 calls    545.3 us   1.62 us/call
  index_elementwise_kernel          112 calls    530.0 us   4.73 us/call   (index_put)
  reduce_kernel                     112 calls    508.7 us   4.54 us/call   (alpha sum)
  remaining elementwise             112 calls    184.1 us   1.64 us/call
  TOTAL                            1568 calls   3578.8 us   2.28 us/call
= 14 kernels per layer x 112 layers. Two corollaries:
  (a) >half the launches gather operands that do not depend on the layer's
      activations (R[rail], v[pidx], S[rail], u[pidx] -- functions of the token only);
  (b) the non-vectorised elementwise rows are taken because noise_buf[:, off:off+d]
      is a strided view (row stride = d_total).

REMAINING HEADROOM
------------------
1. The per-token noise draw, 0.199 ms of the 2.94 ms clean baseline (now ~13% of
   the rail-inclusive step at N=1). draw_noise() materialises an INT64 randint
   buffer (7.34 MB per slot at d_total=917,504) and then runs a 5-kernel
   cast/scale/copy chain: ~42 MB of traffic and ~6 kernels per slot per token.
   Drawing straight into the bf16 buffer, or folding a Philox stream into the
   rail kernel itself, removes most of it. The same routine regenerates noise for
   every token during assembly, so this also cuts the 13.6 s assemble phase.
   Any change must move decode and assembly together -- they must regenerate
   bit-identical noise.
2. pack_width remains the dominant wall-clock lever (see results/zo_opd.md 2):
   the full-context scratch-KV reservation caps concurrency at 4-8 slots.
