Phase analysis
--------------
- decode 128.3 s = 16 waves x ~7.92 s, i.e. ~7.8 ms per token-step for
  36 rows x 28 layers -- AT the memory-bound rail floor (NP isolation OFF-floor
  was ~12.4 ms/tok on A800). The three NP cost centers are gone by
  construction: one fused noise draw per (slot, token) instead of 896
  draw_noise launches (74% of NP decode); no per-token D2H u/x captures
  (payload is a device-buffer gather, one D2H per wave); no per-token full
  cuda.synchronize().
- teacher 4.2 s: sampled-token OPD = ONE prompt_logprobs prefill per rollout.
  10x FASTER than BP's own teacher phase (41.7 s through the FSDP reward
  worker + top-k machinery).
- assemble 13.2 s: seed-regenerated noise + N GEMMs/chunk/layer (~1.5 PFLOP),
  comparable to BP's backward+optimizer (15.0 s). NP's 835 s Python loop is
  gone.
- The remaining 2.3x vs BP is STRUCTURAL, all in decode: the hand-driven
  serial token loop at pack_width=4 yields ~511 tok/s vs vLLM continuous
  batching's 1134 tok/s, and each token runs 1+N=9 rows. Levers, in order:
  pack_width 8+ (teacher on its own GPU, or shrink the full-ctx scratch-KV
  reservation), overlap teacher prefill with the next wave's decode (4.2 s,
  minor now), and the (1+N)-row cost is near-free only to N~16.
